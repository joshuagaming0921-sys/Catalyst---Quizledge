Features

Everything You Need to Teach Smarter

Live Scoreboards
Real-time rankings update as students complete quizzes. Keep the class engaged with competitive, transparent scoring.
XP and Achievements
Students earn experience points for every quiz completed. Unlock badges and level up to stay motivated throughout the course.
Shared Notes
Students upload and share study notes with classmates. Teachers review and approve content before it goes live.
Quiz Builder
Create multi-choice assessments in minutes. Set timers, assign to classes, and track results from your admin dashboard.
Classroom Management
Organize students into classes, manage enrollment, and monitor activity — all from one streamlined admin panel.
Role-based Access
Separate dashboards for teachers and students. Each role sees exactly what they need — nothing more, nothing less.
How It Works

From Sign-Up to Score in Four Steps

01
Create Your Account
Register as a teacher or student. Admins set up classrooms and manage enrollment from day one.
▸
02
Join or Build a Class
Teachers create classrooms and invite students. Students join with a class code and get immediate access.
▸
03
Take Quizzes and Earn XP
Students complete timed quizzes, answer questions, and accumulate experience points for each session.
▸
04
Track Progress and Rank Up
View scores, climb the leaderboard, review past results, and share notes — all in one place.
